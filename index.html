The Studio

Habby Interiors is a design studio for people who appreciate the difference between a space that is simply decorated and one that is thoughtfully crafted. We work closely with every client—from the first consultation to the final styling—ensuring every material, texture, finish, and detail is carefully chosen to create interiors that are timeless, functional, and uniquely yours.

Every project starts with the architecture of the space itself — light, flow, structure — before a single material or furniture piece is chosen.

How We Work

From first walkthrough to final styling.

A fixed sequence, kept flexible enough to fit around your timeline and budget.

01

Discovery & Brief

A walkthrough of the space, a conversation about how you use it, and a clear scope, budget and timeline before any design begins.

02

Concept & Moodboard

Layout options, material palette and furniture direction, presented as a concept you can react to and refine with us.

03

Sourcing & Procurement

Furniture, fittings and finishes sourced or built to spec, with every order tracked against the agreed plan.

04

Installation & Styling

Delivery, placement and final styling — the space handed back to you finished, not just furnished.
